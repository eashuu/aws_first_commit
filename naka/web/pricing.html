Before you ask sales

Because call volume is a measure of how busy you are, and protected identities is a measure of how much you stand to lose. Pricing on calls also creates exactly the wrong incentive: it makes turning the guardrail off in high-traffic paths a cost saving.

Two arm64 Lambdas, on-demand DynamoDB, and per-request charges for the detector tiers you enable. Tier 1 is free — it runs locally and makes no API call. Tier 2 is Comprehend per unit of text. Tier 3 is Bedrock per image and is the only expensive one; it is invoked for documents, not for every call.

Yes. Each plane is independently deployable and most teams start with Agent Egress because that is where the new risk is. The planes get more useful together — a shared ledger means a budget cannot be reset by switching format — but none of them requires the others.

The Self-hosted tier is not a trial, it is the product. Deploy it, run the scenarios, read the audit rows, and decide whether the managed policy plane is worth paying for. Nothing is time-limited and nothing phones home.
